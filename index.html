      Notation and tab

      Bass sounds an octave lower than written. Tab numbers are frets, top line is your highest string.

      Some rhythms mix straight and triplet steps in one beat, so they're shown approximately.

      Grooves

      Tap a pad to load it, tap it again to play. Tap another while one plays and it switches when the loop comes round. Empty pads save the groove in the editor.

      A template plays its rhythm over the chords you've set, replacing this groove's notes. Undo brings them back.
